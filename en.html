Decifra Rótulo explains food labels written in Portuguese and Spanish in plain language. It is ideal if you live in or travel to Spain, Portugal or Brazil and want to know what is really in the food you buy.

How it works

  1. 1Take a photo of the ingredient list, pick an image from your gallery or type the text.
  2. 2Check the text that was read and fix anything that is wrong.
  3. 3See every ingredient explained: what it is, what it is used for and whether it deserves attention.

See the app

What you get

Every ingredient explained

Colourings, preservatives, thickeners, sweeteners, flavour enhancers and more, with E numbers.

Allergen alerts

Gluten, milk, egg, soya, peanuts, nuts, fish, crustaceans and the rest of the 14 EU allergens, plus lactose and latex.

A score from 0 to 10

With the reason for every point taken off, and whether the product is ultra-processed. Warns when sugar or salt is among the first ingredients, about several kinds of sugar and trans fats.

History

Your last 30 analyses are kept only on your phone, and you can clear them whenever you like.

Built for your privacy

  • Works 100% offline: the app does not even have internet permission.
  • No ads, no account, no sign-up, no subscription. Pay once and that is it.
  • No data is collected. Your photo and history stay on your device.
  • Found an ingredient the app does not know? Tap “Let the developer know” and send it from your own email app, only if you want to.
